Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-13

This is a self-hosted instance of Postiz, an open-source social media scheduling tool, operated for personal use.

1. Data We Collect

When you connect a social media account (e.g. TikTok) via OAuth, we store: your account identifier, display name/avatar, and the access/refresh tokens needed to publish content on your behalf. We also store the content (text, images, video) you create or upload for scheduling.

2. How We Use Data

Data is used solely to operate this instance: authenticating your connected accounts, scheduling and publishing posts you create, and displaying analytics returned by the connected platform's API.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data is only transmitted to the social media platforms you explicitly connect, as required to publish your scheduled content.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored in a private database on infrastructure controlled by the operator of this instance. No third-party analytics or advertising trackers are used.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Data is retained until you disconnect the associated social account or request deletion. To request deletion of your data, contact us using the details below.

6. Contact
